# Harbor workspace support policy

Harbor is a fictional team collaboration product used only for this demo.

## Plans and billing
- Starter: $12 per member per month, monthly billing only.
- Team: $20 per member per month, monthly or annual billing.
- Enterprise: custom contract; support must route billing questions to the account team.
- Adding a member to a monthly Starter or Team plan creates a prorated charge for the remaining billing period.
- Removing a member changes the charge at the next renewal; no automatic mid-period refund.
- Annual plans and Enterprise seat changes require the account team; do not invent a proration formula.

## Cancellation and refunds
- A workspace owner can cancel in Settings > Billing > Manage plan.
- Access continues until the end of the paid period. Cancellation does not immediately delete data.
- A refund can be requested within 14 days of the initial purchase, provided no project has been exported.
- For this demo, this rule applies to the initial purchase of either a workspace subscription or a project-capacity add-on. Use the invoice's purchase type and purchase date for that product.
- Renewals are not automatically refundable. Escalate disputed or duplicate charges to billing support.
- Support can explain eligibility but cannot approve or issue a refund in chat.

## Access and security
- Workspace owners and admins may invite members from Settings > Members.
- Only the owner may change billing or transfer ownership.
- If the owner has lost access, escalate to account recovery. Never suggest bypassing ownership checks.
- Never request passwords, API keys, recovery codes, or full card numbers.
- For billing escalation, ask only for the workspace ID, invoice ID, and a short description of the issue.

## Data
- Owners can export projects from Settings > Data > Export.
- After cancellation, data is retained for 30 days after the paid period ends, then deleted.
- Enterprise retention is contractual: ask the account team rather than promising a fixed period.
- A deleted project can be restored by an admin within 7 days via Settings > Data > Recently deleted.
- This document does not specify supported export formats, SSO providers, or service uptime guarantees.
